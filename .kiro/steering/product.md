# Product

## Pitch

A phone makes a 3D shape float above the desk. A teacher prints the same shape as cards. The kit learns each class's level from the lessons the teacher records.

## Users

- Teachers in schools and tuition centres with no projector and few screens.
- Learners who find 3D drawings hard.

## The 30-second test

A teacher who has never seen the kit must, within 30 seconds of opening it, see a shape on screen and know the one thing to tap next. If a change breaks that, it fails.

## Honest lines

- Ghost Desk is a Pepper's ghost illusion for 2 to 4 learners in a dim room. It is not a true hologram.
- Pyramid pseudo-hologram studies in primary schools found more fun and motivation, not more learning. Use it as a shared display and a hook.
- Learning is Elo-style practice statistics (as in Math Garden). It is statistics, not AI. Kiro's AI is used only while building.
- Difficulty is frozen during a lesson and updated at the end.
- Do not claim the kit is unique. Related tools: Plickers, pyramid hologram videos, Math Garden.

## Privacy rules

- Data stays on the device.
- Exports hold counts per item only.
- No names, ages, gender or timestamps in any export.
- No item is exported with fewer than 5 answers.
- Each export has a unique id and a pack hash. A file merged twice counts once.
- No faces of minors on film. Hands and cards only.

## Safety

Clear sheets and pyramids can have sharp edges. Adults cut and fold them.
